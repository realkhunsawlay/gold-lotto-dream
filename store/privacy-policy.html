App: Gold & Lotto (ရွှေဈေး နှင့် ထီ) – Thai gold price & lottery checker
Last updated: 26 September 2026

Summary

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

What the app does

The app downloads the latest Thai gold price from a public price service (api.chnwt.dev), and Thai Government Lottery results from the Government Lottery Office (glo.or.th), with a public backup service (lotto.api.rayriffy.com). Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. The app sends nothing else, and lottery numbers you check are never sent anywhere.

The last downloaded price, the last lottery results, and the ticket numbers you check are saved only on your device, so the app can show them offline and re-check your tickets. You can remove them by deleting the numbers in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app only uses the Internet permission, to download prices.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Disclaimer

Prices and results are for reference only. Always confirm lottery results with the official GLO announcement before claiming a prize. This app is not affiliated with the Gold Traders Association of Thailand or the Government Lottery Office, and it does not sell lottery tickets or accept bets.
